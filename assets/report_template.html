Raw reads per BRK barcode

Reads assigned by torchlex to each inner barcode, stacked by batch. Diamonds show classified reads in the feature table. Faded bars are samples below the read threshold.

Relative abundance, species level

Taxa below the abundance threshold in every shown sample are grouped as “Other”. Taxa not resolved to species are shown at their lowest classified rank.

Rarefaction curves

Expected number of taxa at each sequencing depth, computed analytically without random subsampling. Taxa below the abundance threshold are left out.

Absolute quantification with the λ internal standard (Q16Seq)

Each sample is spiked with one of three λ-phage internal standards. Enter the copies of λ1 added per reaction; λ2 and λ3 are 10× and 100× less. For every taxon: 16S copies = λ copies added × taxon reads / λ reads. Samples are flagged when λ reads are outside 10–90% of all classified reads (range of quantification). Settings are remembered in this browser.
